Mission Underway
Every STEAM discipline — to win

At Learnly, STEAM stops being just a subject and starts being the only way to win. Our curriculum seamlessly integrates every STEAM discipline because our immersive missions demand them. It takes critical thinking, relentless teamwork, and unstoppable problem-solving to win this game!

S
Science
Real experiments, data collection, hypothesis testing — the science unlocks the next step of the build
T
Technology
Code, sensors, drones, DAWs, game engines — tools chosen because the mission requires them
E
Engineering
Build, break, rebuild — iterating under real constraints with materials that have limits
A
Art
Design as function — visual identity, composition, communication — making the solution legible to the world
M
Math
Scale ratios, frequency calculations, probability, grid coordinates — applied to the actual problem, never abstract
🤖
Integrated Tool
AI as a real working tool — not a novelty

AI is woven into every mission as a powerful, real-world tool. Kiddos learn when to utilize it, how to critically evaluate its outputs, and, most importantly, when to push back and rely on their own ingenuity — developing the digital literacy and critical thinking necessary to become unstoppable forward-thinking leaders.

+
Use it
AI assists with research, design critique, code review, mix analysis, colour palettes, story structure
?
Question it
Every AI output is evaluated — what did it get right, what did it miss, do you agree with it?
✗
Push back on it
When the AI is wrong, campers say so and explain why — that habit is rarer and more valuable than knowing how to use the tool

A Day In the Life of a Learnly Camp Kiddo.

From the initial mission briefing that starts the game, to the epic final victory celebration, every single day at Learnly is engineered for impactful learning and an unforgettable adventure. You'll watch your kiddo's morning curiosity evolve into afternoon breakthroughs as they dive into hands-on STEAM challenges, collaborative problem-solving, and AI-powered investigations.
